001 — About

"I started learning programming recently. After my laptop stopped working and I couldn't afford to repair it, I kept building."

I'm Vinit — building KnoriX, an AI learning platform that continuously personalizes teaching using knowledge graphs, adaptive pedagogy, and real-time learning intelligence.

No laptop, no team, no funding. Just GitHub, AI coding tools, and a cloud deployment pipeline — run entirely from a phone. I'd rather ship something real than wait for ideal conditions.

Outside of KnoriX, I've been going deep on knowledge graphs (Neo4j, Cypher, GraphRAG) and shipped a domain-specific RAG tool for the mining industry — live demo here.
